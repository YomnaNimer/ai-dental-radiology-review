# Review Protocol

## Working Title

Artificial Intelligence in Dental Radiology: Evidence Review of Applications in Caries Detection, Periodontal Bone Loss, Periapical Lesions, and CBCT/Oral-Maxillofacial Imaging

## Review Type

Narrative evidence review with structured evidence mapping.

## Objective

To summarize current evidence on AI applications in dental radiology and identify clinically relevant opportunities, limitations, and research gaps.

## Population / Clinical Context

Dental patients undergoing diagnostic imaging, including bitewing, periapical, panoramic, and CBCT imaging.

## Concept

Artificial intelligence, machine learning, deep learning, convolutional neural networks, and computer-aided detection/diagnosis.

## Main Application Areas

1. Caries detection
2. Periodontal bone loss detection
3. Periapical lesion detection, segmentation, or classification
4. CBCT and oral-maxillofacial imaging applications

## Inclusion Criteria

- Peer-reviewed review articles, systematic reviews, meta-analyses, or clinically relevant primary studies
- Studies involving AI, machine learning, or deep learning
- Dental radiology or oral-maxillofacial imaging focus
- English-language papers
- Preferably 2020 onward for current evidence, with older foundational reviews allowed when useful

## Exclusion Criteria

- Non-dental imaging unless directly connected to oral-maxillofacial pathology
- Opinion-only articles without evidence synthesis
- Marketing pages without peer-reviewed evidence
- Studies unrelated to diagnosis, detection, segmentation, classification, or radiographic interpretation

## Outcomes of Interest

- AI task: detection, classification, segmentation, measurement, risk prediction
- Imaging type: bitewing, periapical, panoramic, CBCT, CT, MRI
- Dental condition or structure studied
- Reported model performance
- Clinical relevance
- Limitations and risk of bias
- Need for external validation or prospective clinical testing

## Planned Deliverables

- Evidence table
- Narrative synthesis
- Visual evidence map
- Short clinical interpretation for portfolio use
